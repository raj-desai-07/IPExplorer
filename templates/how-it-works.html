The Full Journey

Five Simple Steps

Everything that happens from the moment you open the page to reading your results

Step 01

Enter an IP Address

Type or paste any public IPv4 or IPv6 address into the search field on the home page. The field accepts the full range of valid formats — dotted decimal IPv4 like 192.168.1.1 or compressed IPv6 notation.

Supports IPv4 & IPv6

Step 02

Click the Lookup Button

Hit the Lookup button (or press Enter) to trigger the search. You'll see a brief pulse animation on the search bar, giving instant visual feedback that your request is being processed.

Instant visual feedback
Step 03

Intelligence Databases Queried

The IP is cross-referenced against multiple geolocation and network intelligence sources. This resolves the address to a geographic region, ISP, autonomous system number (ASN), timezone, and connection type — all in milliseconds.

Multi-source resolution
03
04
Step 04

Results Appear in Cards

The IP Details section slides into view with a smooth fade animation. Up to 18 data points are displayed in a responsive card grid — each card reveals in a staggered sequence so you can take it all in at a glance without visual overload.

18 data points, staggered reveal
Step 05

Explore & Use Your Data

Each card is independently readable. Need your own public IP? Use the How to Find my IP button in the navbar to detect and copy your address directly — then paste it straight into the lookup field to explore your own connection details.
